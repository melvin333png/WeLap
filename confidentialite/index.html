Politique de confidentialité

Dernière mise à jour : Janvier 2025

WeLap collecte certaines données nécessaires au fonctionnement de l'application, notamment l'adresse e-mail, les informations de profil, les contenus publiés, les véhicules ajoutés au garage, les chronos, les interactions dans l'application et, lorsque l'utilisateur l'autorise, les données de localisation nécessaires au chronométrage GPS.

Ces données sont utilisées pour fournir les fonctionnalités de l'application : création de compte, connexion, chronométrage, classements, garage, publications, communauté et abonnement Premium.

WeLap ne vend pas les données personnelles des utilisateurs.

Les données de localisation sont utilisées pour les fonctionnalités liées aux circuits et au chronométrage. Elles ne sont pas utilisées à des fins de suivi publicitaire dans la version actuelle de l'application.

Les photos, vidéos, publications, commentaires et informations de garage sont liés au compte utilisateur afin de permettre l'affichage du profil, du garage et des publications.

Suppression de compte et données : Les utilisateurs peuvent demander la suppression de leur compte ou de leurs données en contactant : contact.welap@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

WeLap ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account and Profile Information

When you create a WeLap account, we collect your email address, username, profile picture, and other profile details you provide.

Vehicle Information

When you add vehicles to your garage, we collect information including make, model, year, horsepower, transmission type, and photos.

Location and GPS Data

With your permission, WeLap collects precise location data via GPS when you use our lap timing features. This data is necessary to calculate and record your lap times. You can disable GPS access through your device settings.

Session and Performance Data

We collect and store data about your driving sessions, including lap times, circuit information, vehicle selection, and performance metrics.

User-Generated Content

WeLap allows you to upload photos, videos, posts, and comments. We collect, store, and display this content as part of your profile and community interactions.

Payment and Subscription Information

If you subscribe to WeLap Premium, your subscription is processed through Apple In-App Purchase. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card or banking information. Apple handles all payment processing securely.

Technical Information

We automatically collect technical data including device model, operating system version, app version, and crash reports to improve app stability and performance.

How We Use Information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its features
  • Create and manage your account
  • Record and display your lap times and performance
  • Enable leaderboards and rankings
  • Facilitate community features and sharing
  • Manage your Premium subscription
  • Analyze app usage and improve user experience
  • Send service updates and support responses
  • Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

Location Data

WeLap uses GPS location data for lap timing and performance tracking. Location data is only collected when you actively initiate a GPS recording session. You can disable GPS access through your device settings. Location data is encrypted during transmission and storage.

User Content

You are responsible for the content you upload to WeLap. When you upload content, you grant WeLap a license to use, display, and distribute that content within the App. User-generated content may be visible to other WeLap users depending on your privacy settings.

Payments and Subscriptions

WeLap Premium subscriptions are processed through Apple In-App Purchase using StoreKit. We do not directly process or store payment information. Apple handles all payment processing securely. We use RevenueCat for subscription management and only receive information about your subscription status and purchase events.

Analytics, Advertising, and Crash Reporting

Analytics

We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with the App. This data is typically anonymized and does not directly identify individual users.

Advertising

The free version of WeLap may display advertisements through Google AdMob. You can opt out of personalized ads through your device settings. Advertisements are not displayed in WeLap Premium.

Crash Reporting

We may use crash reporting tools to collect information about app crashes and technical errors. Crash reports may include device information and app state but do not include personal data.

Third-Party Services

Supabase

WeLap uses Supabase for backend infrastructure and database services. Your account information, garage data, session records, and user-generated content are stored on Supabase's secure servers.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and in-app purchases. RevenueCat receives information about your subscription status but does not have access to your payment information.

Apple App Store and StoreKit

On iOS, WeLap uses Apple's StoreKit framework for in-app purchases and subscription management. Apple processes all payments and stores your subscription information.

Google AdMob

If you use the free version of WeLap, you may see advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers to serve personalized ads.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize most of your personal data within a reasonable timeframe, except where legally required.

Account Deletion and Data Requests

You have the right to request access to your personal data, correct inaccurate information, delete your account, and export your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at contact.welap@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

WeLap is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will promptly delete the information.

Security

WeLap implements security measures to protect your personal information, including encryption of data in transit and at rest, secure authentication, and regular security updates.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy in the App. Your continued use of the App following changes means you accept the new terms.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

Email: contact.welap@gmail.com

We will respond to your inquiry within 30 days.
